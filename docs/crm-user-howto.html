Meow CRM - How To Use (Non-Technical)

This is the simple version. If you can read this, you can run the system.

1) Leads

1
Go to Leads and click Add.
2
Fill in name + contact info. Save.
3
Use Quick Actions to call, text, or email.
4
Pick a Call Outcome and click Mark Contacted.

2) Book a Meeting (Auto Converts)

1
Open the lead and click the Book Meeting tab.
2
Set date, time, and attendees.
3
Click Book. The lead becomes an account + opportunity automatically.

3) Accounts & Opportunities

1
Accounts hold your customers or prospects.
2
Opportunities are the deals (pipeline cards).
3
Move opportunities through stages as you progress.

4) Customers

1
Use Customers to track after the sale.
2
Send updates or campaigns to customers from the same system.

5) Email (Gmail + Mailgun)

1
Connect Gmail in User Settings.
2
Send emails directly from a lead/account/customer.
3
Use Campaigns for bulk email (Mailgun).
4
Click Sync Gmail Inbox to pull inbound replies.

6) Campaigns

1
Go to Campaigns and create a new campaign.
2
Pick the audience (Leads, Customers, Accounts, or Opportunities).
3
Use the builder to design your email.
4
Click Send Now or schedule it.

7) Telemarketer Tools

1
Use call scripts built into Leads.
2
Use the Call Timer if enabled.
3
Set callback date/time on the lead when a contact asks to be called back.
4
Auto‑advance moves you to the next lead after you mark contacted.

8) Admin Settings (Only if you’re Admin)

1
Go to Settings and configure schemas, roles, and integrations.
2
Telemarketer tools can be toggled on/off from Settings.
3
Mailgun + Google Workspace setup is done here.
4
Use the new API Integrations page to connect other services with simple forms.
5
Use callback widget and callback field settings for your lead team workflow.
