How Everything Connects

The system is designed as a single, continuous workflow. Lead capture flows into meeting booking, which triggers account creation and opportunity tracking. Outreach is delivered through campaigns or Gmail, and responses are synced back into the same record. Every stage updates the next.

Lead → Meeting → Account

Booking a meeting automatically creates the account and opportunity with full context attached.

Account → Opportunity

Opportunities stay linked to accounts, maintaining a complete view of commercial activity.

Outbound → Inbound

Campaigns and Gmail logs connect directly to inbound replies for full communication history.
